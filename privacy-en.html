Privacy policy

Over It is an app for counting time without a habit. It works entirely on your device and does not send data anywhere.

1. What data the app holds

Counters: the habit name, the start date and time, the history of periods, the reasons you wrote down, cost and quantity settings. App settings: language, theme, background, reminders. A photo, if you chose one as the background. All of this is stored only in your phone’s memory.

2. What the app does not do

It does not require registration or an account. It does not collect analytics or usage statistics. It does not show ads and contains no advertising trackers. It does not send data to the developer’s servers or to third parties. The app does not access the internet.

3. Permissions

Notifications: needed for reminders and achievement messages. All notifications are created on the device itself, without a server.

Photo library: needed only if you want to choose your own photo as the background or save a “Share” card. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.

4. Export and import

You can export your data to a file and import it back. The file is created on your device, and you decide where to keep it and who to share it with.

5. Purchases

Premium features are paid for through the app store (App Store, Google Play or RuStore). Payment data is processed by the store. The developer does not receive your card number or other payment details, only a purchase confirmation from the store.

6. Deleting data

All data is deleted together with the app. Individual counters can be deleted within the app.

7. Age

The app is not intended for persons under 18.

8. Changes

If the policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on this page with a new date.
